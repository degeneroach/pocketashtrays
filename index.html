Why cities use pocket ashtrays

Cigarette butts are the most littered item on our streets

Cigarette filters are made of plastic (cellulose acetate), break down slowly and leach chemicals into soil and waterways. Most of them end up on the ground simply because there is no bin nearby. A pocket ashtray gives every smoker a bin they carry with them.

Municipalities, parks boards and business improvement areas hand out branded pocket ashtrays as part of litter-reduction campaigns, smoke-free bylaw rollouts, beach clean-ups and storm-drain protection programs. Every unit is a cleaner sidewalk and a reminder of who is taking care of the community.

FAQ

Custom pocket ashtray questions

What is a pocket ashtray?

A pocket ashtray is a small, reusable, fire-resistant pouch that lets smokers extinguish and carry cigarette butts until they reach a proper bin, keeping filters out of streets, storm drains, parks and beaches.

What is the minimum order for custom pocket ashtrays?

We print in-house on a UV flatbed printer, so we can handle small runs for events and local businesses as well as large-volume programs for cities and corporate accounts. Email us at custompocketashtray@gmail.com with your quantity for pricing.

Can you print our full-colour logo?

Yes. We print full-colour artwork, gradients and fine detail directly onto the pouch with UV-cured ink. We send a digital mock-up for your approval before anything is printed.

Do you work with municipalities and public tenders?

Yes. Cities, regional districts, parks departments and business improvement areas are our primary focus. We can provide formal quotes, volume pricing and scheduled releases for multi-phase programs.

How long does production take?

Turnaround depends on quantity and the time of year. Small runs are typically quick once your mock-up is approved, and large program orders are scheduled with you up front. Need it fast? We can accommodate last-minute rush orders, just tell us your deadline.
